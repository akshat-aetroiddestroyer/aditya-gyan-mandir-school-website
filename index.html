Aditya Gyan Mandir School (AGMS) is a premier co-educational institution established in 1997 and run by the Samiti. Founded with a deep commitment to accessible quality education, our campus features a comprehensive library with more than 2,000 books, dedicated Biology and Chemistry laboratories, and a modern Computer Laboratory. Guided by highly qualified and devoted teachers, generations of AGMS alumni have gone on to achieve excellence and hold distinguished positions in their professional careers.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Guided by purpose, inspiring every step.

Our official Vision, Mission, and Goals form the cornerstone of every classroom discussion, laboratory discovery, and co-scholastic opportunity at Aditya Gyan Mandir School.

Unlocking Every Student's Potential

School Vision

"We believe that all students can learn and that students' learning potential is unknown and not pre-determined. We view education as a profession in which practice and theory interact continually and dynamically to guide curriculum, pedagogy, and educational leadership."

Official School Vision
Equity, Excellence & Reflective Practice

School Mission

"Our programs emphasize equity, academic excellence, reflective practice, curriculum integration, and advocacy. Our programs promote meaningful fieldwork in which pre-service and in-service teachers engage with students, families, and other educators within diverse communities. Through their courses and fieldwork, our students develop the dispositions that guide and support their teaching and leading."

Official School Mission
Learning & Leading Across Horizons

School Goals

"To actualize our vision and mission, we engage with our students in the classroom and in the field to learn and lead in a wide range of local, state, regional, national, and international settings."

Official School Goals
